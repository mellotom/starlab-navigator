buttons · LEDs

sl_01
[ infinite ]
freezes what’s going into the reverb · new sound keeps passing through

shimmer inside the reverb, it climbs · off = shimmer on the input only

glimmer lifts the high harmonics · off = the low ones

07

lfo

sl_07
[ lfo target ]
delay chorus, flange, vibrato (echo on for more) · pitch size / pitch · filter high damp (low pass on for sweeps)
[ speed ]
15 s to 15 hz · env: the decay time
[ shape ]
triangle · square · ramp · saw · random · env (follows your input)
[ depth ]
off fully ccw · env: off at noon, ccw negative, cw positive
[ lfo clk in ]
locks to a clock · speed picks the ratio (fig.02)
[ ext lfo in ]
your own modulation to the target · depth becomes its attenuverter, off at noon
